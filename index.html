COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions about invoice and purchase order checking.

What is invoice matching software?

Invoice matching software compares supplier invoice details with related purchasing records, such as a purchase order or quotation. Matchqip highlights differences in the documents so a user can investigate them.

Can Matchqip check an invoice against a purchase order?

Yes. Add the related documents, assign them to the same purchase number and run the comparison. Matchqip highlights differences in the information it extracts from the files.

Does Matchqip find price and quantity discrepancies?

Matchqip checks for differences in unit prices, line amounts and quantities across related documents. It can also flag repeated line items, additional charges and calculation inconsistencies.

Can Matchqip help with accounts payable invoice verification?

Matchqip can be used as an additional document review step in accounts payable and procurement workflows. It does not replace your accounting system, approval controls or payment process.

Which file types can I check?

Matchqip supports PDF, DOCX, XLSX and TXT files. The quality of the comparison depends on the structure and readable content of the documents.

Does Matchqip upload supplier documents?

Matchqip is designed to run locally on the Windows device. Review the privacy policy for details about document processing and information stored on the computer.
