WHAT MATCHQIP CHECKS

Bring document differences into view.

Important changes can be easy to overlook when supplier documents are reviewed separately. Matchqip compares the information it can extract and flags differences for closer examination.

01

Price discrepancies

Identify differences in unit prices and line amounts across related supplier documents.

02

Quantity differences

Compare quantities and bring mismatches between the order and invoice to your attention.

03

Duplicate charges

Highlight repeated line items or charges that may need a second look.

04

Additional fees

Review charges that appear on an invoice but may not be present in the source documents.

05

Calculation checks

Flag arithmetic inconsistencies in line totals, subtotals, taxes and grand totals.

06

Evidence for review

Inspect findings alongside the extracted document information used in the comparison.

A PRACTICAL REVIEW STEP

From supplier documents to a clearer review.

Matchqip adds a document-comparison step to your existing purchasing or accounts-payable process. It supports review; it does not replace your accounting platform, approval policy or professional judgement.

2
