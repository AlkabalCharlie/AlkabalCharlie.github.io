WHAT MATCHQIP CHECKS

Find the differences that matter.

Supplier documents can look consistent at a glance. Matchqip helps bring important differences into view so your team can investigate them.

01

Price discrepancies

Highlight differences in unit prices and line amounts between source documents.

02

Quantity differences

Compare quantities and identify where the invoice does not match the order.

03

Duplicate charges

Bring repeated line items or charges to your attention for review.

04

Additional fees

Spot charges that may not appear in the quotation or purchase order.

05

Calculation checks

Flag arithmetic inconsistencies in line totals, subtotals, taxes and grand totals.

06

Evidence in context

Review findings alongside the relevant source-document information.
